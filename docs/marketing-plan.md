# 90-Day Marketing Plan — EFK Battles

> Source: marketing-plan skill. AARRR-structured growth plan for the first quarter of operations.

## Context
- Product: mobile-only eFootball 1v1 tournament platform, M-Pesa + WhatsApp, Blaze partnership
- Offer: KES 100 entry, 32 players, KES 1,600 first prize
- Cadence: 3 tournaments/week (Mon/Wed/Fri 8 PM EAT)

## North Star Metric
**Paid players per week.** Secondary: bracket fill rate (paid÷32) and repeat-entry rate.

## AARRR Plan

### Acquisition
- WhatsApp Channel as owned distribution hub (fastest: fixtures live)
- Instagram/TikTok creative engine (3 posts/day per social-calendar.md)
- Meta ads: conversions pixel on register, manual bidding KES CPL target (ad-playbook.md)
- Referral loop: success screen WhatsApp-share button + "bring a friend and 2 of you cover 2 spots" coding option
- Public live bracket = shareable proof (deep link /live)

### Activation
- Registration → M-Pesa in ~30 seconds ("Sending STK push" state)
- First-match activation: WhatsApp welcome message with how-to link before kick-off
- Reduce no-shows with 90-min and 15-min reminder messages (cron + WhatsApp)

### Retention
- Weekly recurring tournament = built-in hook
- Leaderboard / "streak" badge for consecutive tournaments (admin field)
- WhatsApp group per tournament for banter + reshare

### Referral
- "Refer a player" share box already on the success screen
- Track via UTM/referral short link in share text; reward top referrers with a free entry credit

### Revenue
- KES 2,880 gross per tournament (32 x 100 = 3,200 - 0 payout risk; platform take 960)
- Path to scale: 2 tournaments/day on weekends, "double pot" weekends (64 players), merch/kit resell, Blaze-funded community events

## 90-Day Roadmap
| Weeks | Focus | Deliverable |
|-------|-------|-------------|
| 1-2 | Foundation | Supabase live, 3 test tournaments, 20 test players, WhatsApp templates approved |
| 3-4 | First 100 players | Launch social engine + WhatsApp channel, 200-300 spend, 3 paid tournaments |
| 5-8 | Prove repeat | 10+ sold-out tournaments, referral loop live, leaderboard/streak feature |
| 9-12 | Scale | Meta conversions campaigns, weekend double-pot, partner content with Blaze |

## Weekly Operating Rhythm
- Mon/Wed/Fri 16:00: countdown posts + boost top performer
- After each final: payout proof post (Sat 11:00 recap)
- Sunday: week review — CPPP, fill rate, no-show rate, retention

## Team
- 1 operator/admin (matches, disputes) + content creator (3 posts/day) + fractional media buyer (ad-playbook). All part-time in Kenya.
