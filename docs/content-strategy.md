# Content Strategy — EFK Battles

> Source: content-strategy skill. What to create, for whom, and in what order.

## Audience
- Primary: Kenyan mobile gamers 18-25, Safaricom subscribers, eFootball Mobile players
- Problem: no organized, trusted, real-money eFootball Mobile tournaments in Kenya
- Objection: "Is this a scam?" → trust content required; "Can I actually win?" → proof content required

## Content Pillars

| Pillar | Purpose | Formats | Frequency |
|--------|---------|---------|-----------|
| **Tournament launches** | Conversions | Landing, WhatsApp blast, social | 3x/week (Mon/Wed/Fri) |
| **Player wins & proof** | Trust | Pay-ouT screenshots, winner story, bracket screenshots | 3x/week |
| **How-to / education** | Friction removal | How-to-Play, FAQ, short video, checklist | 1x/week |
| **Community & banter** | Retention | Polls, rivalries, clips, WhatsApp groups | Daily |
| **Blaze partnership** | Brand safety | Sponsor mentions, event tags, photo of kit | 1x/week |

## Topic Cluster (SEO-friendly)
- The money tournament ("eFootball tournaments that pay real money in Kenya")
- Match format explainer (rules, no-show, disputes)
- M-Pesa payment walkthrough
- Efootball Mobile tips for beginners
- Monthly "biggest wins" recap

## Funnel Mapping

| Stage | Content | CTA |
|-------|---------|-----|
| Awareness | Reels/clips of finals, win payouts | Follow + WhatsApp channel |
| Consideration | How-to, FAQ, trust bar, real winner stories | Visit /how-to-play |
| Conversion | Scarcity landing, register page | Register → M-Pesa |
| Retention | WhatsApp reminders, bracket live page, next-tournament join | Join next round |
| Advocacy | Referral share button (in success screen) | Share on WhatsApp |

## Editorial Calendar Cadence
- Mon/Wed/Fri: pre-tournament countdown posts (4pm, then 7pm "1 hour to kick-off")
- Wed: educational post (rotate how-to/FAQ/rules)
- Fri: winner announcement + payout proof (Sat morning recap)
- Run a weekly 4-post hook for a 4-week test cycle before scaling

## Measurement
- Funnel: signups → M-Pesa converted → bracket fill rate
- Channels: WhatsApp channel reach, Instagram/TikTok saves
- Content: CTR on "Join for KES 100" links tagged per platform
