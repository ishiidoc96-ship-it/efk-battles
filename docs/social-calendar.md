- **WhatsApp Channel**: fixtures, room codes, results, polls — operational hub

## 30-Second Reel Template (repeatable)
1. (0-3s) Hook: "KES 100 in. KES 1,600 out. Tonight."
2. (3-10s) Phone gameplay clip + on-screen score
3. (10-18s) Brackets locking, WhatsApp notification pop
4. (18-25s) Winner celebration + M-Pesa notification
5. (25-30s) "Register for Monday — link in bio"

## Production Notes
- Always brand-safe: no real loser humiliation, no gambling language, skill-based framing
- Re-use the regenerate-able win proof screenshots (blur other names / phone numbers)
- Keep Blaze logo present on every asset but never recolored
