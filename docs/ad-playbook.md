# Ad Playbook — EFK Battles

> Source: ads skill. Paid acquisition strategy for a KES 100 eFootball tournament. Small budget, high-velocity, geo-targeted Kenya.

## Why ads (not just organic)
- Tournament fills in hours — ads plug directly into the 32-spot scarcity loop
- KES 100 entry means low CPL target: pay up to ~KES 50-80 per confirmed registration and you still cover the platform take (KES 960) per sold-out tournament

## Platforms

### 1. Meta (Instagram + Facebook) — primary
- Objective: "Conversions" (website registration pixel), fallback "Traffic" with UTMs
- Audience: Kenya 18-25, interests: eFootball/PES/FIFA, mobile gaming, football (Gor Mahia, AFC Leopards, Premier League, Harambee Stars), gaming content
- Exclusions: gambling intent; age <18 (policy-safe); rural areas with no Safaricom
- Placement: Instagram Reels + Stories feed first (cost-efficient), Feed second
- Creative rotation: Scoreboard (CTR) vs Payout Proof (conversion) vs Blaze Dark countdown

### 2. TikTok — organic first, paid boost on winner videos
- Spark Ads on winning/champion clips to amplify social proof
- Native "Link in bio" → register page

### 3. Google Search (later) — long-tail
- "eFootball tournament Kenya", "play eFootball for money", "M-Pesa esports"
- Low volume, high intent, cheap CPC

## Budget ladder (KES)
| Phase | Weekly | Focus |
|-------|--------|-------|
| Test | 2,000 | Meta Reels, 2 creatives, broad audience |
| Scale winner | 5,000 | Winning angle, lookalike 1% of registrants |
| Sustain | 10,000-15,000 | Full-funnel, always-on countdown ads |

## Tracking & measurement
- Pixel event: "Registration" (form submitted) + "Payment" (server-side, on success poll)
- UTMs per creative: fb_countdown, fb_payout, tiktok_winner, google_brand
- Dashboard asks: cost per paid player (CPPP), bracket fill rate, fee-waived vs paid ratio
- Rigid KPI: CPPP ≤ KES 80 (20% of pot headroom)

## Compliance
- Meta prohibits misleading payout claims: use "up to KES 1,600" if shown
- No gambling framing; position as skill-based esports
- Age targeting policy compliant; link terms + disclaimer in every ad set description
- Use only official Blaze/M-Pesa logos per banner-specs
