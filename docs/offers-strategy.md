# Offer Strategy — EFK Battles

> Source: offers skill. How the KES 100 entry offer is structured to convert and protect the platform.

## The Core Offer

| Element | Value |
|---------|-------|
| Entry | KES 100 per player |
| Field | 32 players, single-elimination |
| 1st place | 50% of pot = KES 1,600 |
| 2nd place | 20% of pot = KES 640 |
| Platform | 30% = KES 960 |
| Format | 1v1 on eFootball Mobile, phone-only |

## Offer Anatomy

### 1. Value framing (already in copy)
- Outcome-first: "Winner takes KES 1,600 — paid to M-Pesa within 24h"
- Risk is tiny (KES 100), upside is clear (16x on first place)
- Reference anchor: it's the price of a soda/lunch — "cheaper than a round of mandazi"

### 2. Bonus stack (adding value, not price)
Web copy should surface these "bonuses" that are already in the product:
- Free WhatsApp fixture/room-code delivery (normally manual relay)
- Auto-bracket generation — no arranging required
- Anti-cheat verification (screenshot matching)
- Admin dispute resolution at no cost

### 3. Guarantee / risk reversal (legal-safe version)
- Full refund if the tournament is cancelled
- Walkover protection: if your opponent no-shows, you win 3-0 automatically
- Payment failure = no charge (verified via transaction status)
- CAK complaint route (regulator link in footer) — builds trust

### 4. Scarcity & urgency (disabled at sensor-verified capacity)
- "Only 32 spots" + "First come, first serve" + live spots-left counter
- Countdown to next tournament at 8 PM EAT on the landing page
- CTA shifts from "Join Now" → "Only N Left" as spots drop below 10

## Payout Integrity (protected by design)
- 80% of the pot goes to players (50% + 20%), 30% funds operations — stated openly
- Prizes paid to the SAME Safaricom number used to register
- Anti-fraud: screenshot verification + screen recording required in semis/final

## Recommended A/B Tests
1. CTA text: "Pay KES 100 with M-Pesa" vs "Lock My Spot — KES 100"
2. Waitlist CTA vs "Notify me for next tournament"
3. Hero stat order: Winner ($1,600) already first — test "Your entry = KES 100" as first row

## Risks to avoid
- Never claim "guaranteed prize" or "guaranteed win"
- Do not call it "50/50" payout (it's 80/20 split + platform)
- Never imply Safaricom endorses the bets/gambling framing — it's skill-based tournament entry
